How to choose a rolling cart for a small bathroom

  • Measure the width and depth of the gap beside the vanity, toilet or tub, and add a little room for the wheels
  • Check that the bathroom door can open fully with the cart in place
  • Look at your floor, since a smooth tile floor is easier for wheels than uneven grout lines
  • Keep heavy items on the lower tier, and light items on top
  • Check that the wheels lock before you load the cart
  • Wipe the cart dry after use, since a bathroom is damp

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a rolling utility cart in a bathroom?

Yes, if it fits the space and you keep it dry. Check what the listing says about the material and finish, and wipe it dry after use.

How narrow a gap does a rolling bathroom cart need?

Measure the gap and compare it with the overall size and the wheel details on the product page. Leave a little room so the cart rolls in and out.

Are rolling carts good for towels and toiletries?

Often yes. Keep towels and light items on the upper tiers and heavier bottles on the lower tier, and check the weight rating.
