How to measure for an under-sink organizer

  • Measure the width and height of the cabinet opening, and the depth to the back wall
  • Note where the pipes, the shut-off valves and any garbage disposal sit
  • Check that the door opens fully and the drawer can slide out past it
  • Think about what you will store, such as cleaning products or toiletries
  • Keep heavy bottles on the lower tier
  • Check the listing for how the organizer sits, and whether it needs tools

What to check before buying

  • Cabinet opening size and pipe positions
  • Listed size of the organizer and the pack count
  • How the tiers stack and slide
  • Weight guidance on the product page

Frequently asked questions

Will an under-sink organizer fit around pipes?

Many are designed to work around pipes, but cabinets differ. Measure the opening and where the pipes sit, then compare with the listed size.

Can I use the same organizer in the kitchen and the bathroom?

Often yes. Check the product page for the rooms it is listed for, and the size against your cabinet.

Do I need tools to set up an under-sink organizer?

Some need little or no tools. Read the setup details on the product page before you order.
