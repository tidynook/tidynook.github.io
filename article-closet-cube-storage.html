How to choose a cube storage shelf

  • Measure the wall space, and the height up to the ceiling or any shelf above
  • Check the product page for how each shelf can be set up, such as upright, on its side or stacked
  • If you want bins, compare the cube opening with the bin size before you buy
  • Put heavier items in the lower cubes for stability
  • Use any anti-tip hardware that is included, especially in a child's room
  • Check whether the back is open or closed, which changes how the wall behind shows

What to check before buying

  • Overall size against your wall space
  • Cube opening against the bins you plan to use
  • How the shelf can be set up, such as stacked, upright or on its side
  • Anti-tip hardware and assembly details
  • Weight rating of the shelves
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a cube storage shelf inside a closet?

Yes, if it fits the closet space. Measure the opening and the depth, and compare them with the overall size on the product page.

Should I attach a cube shelf to the wall?

Tall shelves can tip, so use any anti-tip hardware that comes with the shelf, and follow the instructions, especially in a child's room.

Do cube shelves work with fabric bins?

Often they do, but compare the cube opening with the bin size listed on both product pages before you buy.
