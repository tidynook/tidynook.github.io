How to pack with cubes

  • Give each cube one job, such as tops, bottoms, underwear or chargers
  • Roll or fold clothes the same way each time so the cube closes easily
  • Put the heaviest cube at the bottom, near the wheels or the back panel
  • Keep one slim cube for items you need during the trip
  • Do not overfill, since a stuffed cube makes the bag harder to close

What to check before buying

  • Cube sizes against the inside of your suitcase or backpack

Frequently asked questions

How many packing cubes do I need for a solo trip?

For a carry-on trip, three or four cubes are usually enough. A larger set helps if you like to sort small items separately.

Do packing cubes save space?

They mainly keep your bag organized. Some styles are designed to compress or compact clothes, so check the product page for what each set offers.

Can I use packing cubes in a backpack?

Yes, but measure first. Rigid or large cubes may not fit a narrow backpack, so compare the listed sizes with your bag.
