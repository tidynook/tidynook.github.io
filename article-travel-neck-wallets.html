Tips for carrying documents safely

  • Keep a photo of your passport and cards in a separate secure place
  • Carry only the cards and cash you need for the day
  • Put your passport back in the same pocket every time
  • Keep the wallet zipped when walking in crowds
  • Leave spare cards in your hotel safe or main bag

What to check before buying

  • Fits your passport and the cards you carry
  • Strap length and comfort
  • Zipper and stitching quality in recent reviews
  • Whether you want it worn hidden or visible
  • Bulk under clothing, especially in warm weather
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

Is a neck wallet safe for travel?

It keeps documents close, but no wallet makes theft impossible. Wearing it under clothing and keeping it zipped helps.

Do I need a passport holder if I have a neck wallet?

Not always. A neck wallet can hold a passport, but a slim holder is handy for airports and check-ins.

Does RFID blocking matter?

Products list RFID blocking as a feature, but it is only one part of staying safe. Keeping your documents close and zipped matters too.
