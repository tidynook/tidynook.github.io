How to pack a toiletry bag

  • Decant liquids into small bottles and label them
  • Place bottles upright with caps tightened
  • Put a small zip bag inside for anything that could leak
  • Keep daily items on top and backups at the bottom
  • Let the bag dry fully before you pack it away

What to check before buying

  • Packed size against your carry-on or backpack
  • Number and layout of compartments
  • Zipper quality and recent buyer reviews
  • Whether you need to hang it
  • Airline liquid rules for your route
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

What size toiletry bag do I need for a solo trip?

For a short carry-on trip, a small or medium bag is usually enough. Choose a larger one if you carry full-size bottles.

Is a hanging toiletry bag worth it?

It helps when counter space is limited, such as in hostels or small hotel bathrooms. If you pack light, a compact bag may be enough.

Can I take a toiletry bag in a carry-on?

Yes, but liquid rules differ by airline and country, so check the airport guidance for your route.
