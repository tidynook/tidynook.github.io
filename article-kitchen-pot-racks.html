How to choose a hanging pot rack

  • Decide between a wall mounted and a ceiling mounted rack, based on your kitchen layout
  • Add up the weight of the pots and pans you plan to hang and compare it with the weight rating on the product page
  • Find the studs in the wall or the joists in the ceiling, since heavy cookware needs a secure fixing
  • Leave enough head room, so nobody bumps the pans
  • Check how many hooks are included, and whether you need more
  • If you rent, check what you are allowed to fix to the wall or ceiling

What to check before buying

  • Wall or ceiling mounting and what your kitchen allows
  • Weight rating against your heaviest pan
  • Number and type of hooks included
  • Mounting hardware and installation steps
  • Overall size against your wall or ceiling space
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

Is a wall mounted or a ceiling mounted pot rack better?

It depends on your kitchen. A wall mounted rack suits free wall space, while a ceiling mounted rack uses the space above a counter or island.

Do I need to find studs or joists to hang a pot rack?

Pots and pans are heavy, so a secure fixing matters. Check the installation steps on the product page, and find the studs or joists before you mount it.

How many pots can a hanging rack hold?

It depends on the rack and on how heavy your cookware is. Check the weight rating and the number of hooks on the product page.
