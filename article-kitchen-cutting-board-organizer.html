How to choose a cutting board and baking tray organizer rack

  • Measure the width, depth and height of the space where the organizer will sit
  • Measure your largest cutting board and baking tray, and compare them with the organizer
  • Check on each product page where the organizer goes, such as on a shelf or under a cabinet
  • Make sure the cabinet door can close with the organizer in place
  • Check how many boards and trays the organizer is made to hold
  • Check the installation steps on the product page, and whether tools are needed

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure for a cutting board and baking tray organizer rack?

Measure the width, depth and height of the space where it will sit, and the size of your largest cutting board and tray. Then compare your numbers with the details on the product page.

Where does a cutting board and baking tray organizer go?

Check the product page for how each organizer is placed, for example on a shelf or under a cabinet, and measure that space first.

Can I store cutting boards and baking trays in the same organizer?

Some organizers are listed for both. Check the product page and compare the slots with your boards and trays.
