How to choose a pan and lid organizer rack

  • Measure the width, depth and height of the space where the rack or organizer will sit
  • Check the size of your largest lid against the rack or the dividers
  • Decide whether you want fixed slots or dividers you can adjust
  • Make sure the cabinet door can close with the organizer in place
  • Check how the organizer is placed or fixed, and whether tools are needed
  • Keep the lids you use most within easy reach

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure a cabinet for a pan and lid organizer rack?

Measure the width, depth and height of the space where the rack will sit, and check that the cabinet door can close. Then compare your numbers with the details on the product page.

Will a lid organizer fit lids of different shapes?

Check the product page for the slots or dividers, and compare them with your largest lid. Adjustable dividers let you change the spacing between lids.

Should I choose a rack or an organizer with adjustable dividers?

A rack gives each lid its own slot, while adjustable dividers let you change the spacing to suit your lids. Check the product page and measure your cabinet first.
