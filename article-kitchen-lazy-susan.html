How to choose a lazy susan for a kitchen cabinet

  • Measure the width and depth of the shelf where the turntable will sit
  • Measure the height between shelves, especially if you want a 2-tier turntable
  • Check that the door opens and closes with the turntable and your items in place
  • Keep the tallest items in the middle and the lighter ones near the edge, so it spins smoothly
  • Check whether the surface is non-skid, so bottles do not slide
  • For a corner cabinet, measure the opening itself, since the door frame can limit what fits

Frequently asked questions

Does a lazy susan work in a corner cabinet?

A round turntable can work if it fits the opening. Measure the opening itself, because a door frame can limit the space, and a corner cabinet may need a different shape.

What should I store on a lazy susan?

Spices, oils, sauces, snacks and small jars suit it well. Keep tall items in the middle and lighter items near the edge.

Is a 2-tier lazy susan better than a single one?

A 2-tier turntable uses the height of the shelf, but needs space between shelves. A single turntable is simpler if your shelves are tight.
