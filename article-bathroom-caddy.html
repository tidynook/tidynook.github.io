How to stop a shower caddy from falling down

  • Clean and dry the wall completely before sticking anything on, and remove soap film first
  • Press firmly for the time the instructions give, and wait before loading it with bottles
  • Avoid textured tile or deep grout lines, where the adhesive cannot grip evenly
  • Keep heavy bottles low and spread the weight across several caddies
  • Do not exceed the listed weight capacity
  • If it slips after a few weeks, remove it, clean the wall and try again with fresh adhesive

Frequently asked questions

Do adhesive shower caddies damage tiles?

They usually come off without drilling, but removing adhesive can leave residue or mark some surfaces. If you rent, check the product page and test a small area first.

Will stainless steel caddies rust?

Stainless steel resists rust better than plain metal, but it is not a guarantee in every bathroom. Read recent buyer reviews on how the finish holds up after a few weeks.

How long does the adhesive last?

It depends on the surface, the steam and how much weight you put on it. Buyer reviews are the best guide to how long it lasts in a real shower.
