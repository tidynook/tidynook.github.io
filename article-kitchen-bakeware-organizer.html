How to choose a bakeware organizer rack for a cabinet

  • Measure the width of the cabinet opening at its narrowest point, since a door or face frame can reduce the space
  • Measure the depth from the front of the cabinet to the back wall
  • Measure the height of your sheet pans and the height between shelves
  • Check the size of your largest lid against the rack, and the length of your cutting boards and trays against the organizer
  • Make sure the cabinet door can close with the organizer in place
  • Check the installation steps on the product page, and whether tools are needed

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure a cabinet for a pull-out bakeware organizer?

Measure the width of the cabinet opening at its narrowest point, the depth to the back wall and the height available for sheet pans. Then compare your numbers with the details on the product page.

Will a pot and pan lid rack fit inside my cabinet?

That depends on the cabinet and the rack. Measure the space where it will sit, including the door, and compare it with the details on the product page.

Can I store cutting boards and baking trays together?

An organizer listed for both cutting boards and baking trays is made for that. Measure your boards and trays and compare them with the details on the product page.

Where should I store baking sheets and sheet pans in a small kitchen?

A pull-out organizer lets you slide sheet pans forward so you can reach them without unstacking, as long as the cabinet opening and depth suit it. Check the details on the product page.
