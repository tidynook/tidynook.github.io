How to choose a jewelry armoire

  • Decide between a jewelry box for a dresser or vanity top and a wall or door mounted armoire, based on your space
  • Measure the dresser or vanity top, or the wall or door where it will hang, and the height
  • Check how it is fixed in place, especially a wall or door mounted unit
  • Count your necklaces, earrings and rings, and compare them with the storage layout listed
  • Check the lock details on each listing if you want to keep valuables out of sight
  • Think about whether you want a built-in mirror, as the wall or door mounted option lists one

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose a jewelry box or a wall mounted jewelry armoire?

A jewelry box needs surface space on a dresser or vanity but no wall fixing. A wall or door mounted armoire saves surface and floor space but needs a suitable wall or door.

Can a jewelry organizer be locked?

The wall or door mounted armoire is listed as lockable. Check each product page for how the lock works, and whether the box has one.

How do I know if the storage fits my jewelry?

Count your necklaces, earrings and rings, and compare them with the storage layout described on the product page.
