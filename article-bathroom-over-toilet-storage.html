How to measure for over-the-toilet storage

  • Measure the width of the toilet tank and the space on both sides of it
  • Measure from the floor to the ceiling, and note any window, vent or light switch above the toilet
  • Check how far the unit sticks out compared with the room, so it does not crowd the doorway
  • Leave room to lift the lid and reach the flush handle
  • Make sure the legs or base do not touch the toilet or the wall in a way that blocks cleaning
  • Keep heavier items on lower shelves for stability

What to check before buying

  • Toilet and tank width against the width of the unit
  • Ceiling height against the listed height
  • Whether doors have room to open
  • Weight rating of each shelf and any anti-tip fitting
  • Recent buyer reviews on stability and assembly
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

Will an over-the-toilet cabinet fit around my toilet?

Compare the width and depth listed on the product page with the space around your tank, and make sure you can still lift the lid and reach the flush handle.

Do I need to drill into the wall?

Many over-the-toilet units stand on the floor, but some include an anti-tip strap or fitting that attaches to the wall. Check the product page, and if you rent, confirm what is allowed.

Which is better, an open rack or a cabinet with doors?

A cabinet hides clutter and suits shared or guest bathrooms. An open rack keeps towels and daily items easy to reach.
