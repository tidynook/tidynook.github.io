How to measure for a closet organizer system

  • Measure the closet width from wall to wall, and the height from the floor to the ceiling
  • Measure the depth, and note the door frame and any shelf you plan to remove
  • Find out what the wall is made of, and where the studs are, before you plan the mounting
  • Decide how much hanging space and how many shelves you need
  • Hang your longest garment and measure it, so the rods sit at the right height
  • Compare your measurements with the width range on the product page

What to check before buying

  • Closet width and height against the listed range
  • Wall type, stud position and the mounting steps
  • Weight rating of the shelves and rods
  • How many shelves and rods are included
  • Assembly and installation details on the Amazon page
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which closet system size to buy?

Measure the closet width wall to wall, then compare it with the width range on the product page. Pick the version that covers your closet.

Do closet organizer systems need drilling?

Many wall-mounted systems attach to the wall. Check the installation steps on the product page, and if you rent, confirm what is allowed before you buy.

Can I install a closet system myself?

Many include hardware and instructions. Read the installation steps on the product page, and plan some time and basic tools.
