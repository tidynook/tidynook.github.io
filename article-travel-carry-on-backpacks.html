How to pack a carry-on backpack

  • Put heavier items close to your back and near the middle
  • Use packing cubes to keep clothes in zones
  • Keep documents and chargers in an easy-reach pocket
  • Wear your bulkiest shoes and jacket on travel day
  • Leave a little room so the bag closes without strain

What to check before buying

  • Dimensions against your airline's carry-on rules
  • Empty weight and capacity
  • Strap padding and back panel comfort
  • Opening style, such as clamshell or top-loading
  • Zipper quality and recent buyer reviews
  • Current size and details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

What size is a carry-on backpack?

It varies by airline, so check the size and weight limits for the airlines you fly.

Is 40L too big for a carry-on?

It can be on strict airlines. Check the listed dimensions as well as the litre size.

Can I use packing cubes in a carry-on backpack?

Yes, but measure first. Cubes that are too large may not fit a narrow bag.
