How to measure for an entryway shoe cabinet

  • Measure the width and depth of the wall space, and leave room to walk past
  • Check that flip drawers can tilt open without hitting the door or a radiator
  • Measure your tallest shoes, such as boots, against the drawer height
  • Count how many pairs you want to store and compare with the listed capacity
  • Tall, narrow furniture can tip, so use any anti-tip fitting that is included
  • Check that the top surface can hold what you plan to put on it

What to check before buying

  • Wall width and depth against the listed size
  • Drawer height against your tallest shoes
  • Capacity listed in pairs compared with your own shoes
  • Whether an anti-tip fitting is included
  • Recent buyer reviews on assembly and sturdiness

Frequently asked questions

Are flip drawers better than doors for a narrow entryway?

Flip drawers tilt forward and down, so they need less clearance than doors that swing open. That makes them handy in tight hallways.

How many pairs of shoes will a cabinet hold?

It depends on the model and your shoe sizes. Check the pair count listed on the product page and compare it with the shoes you need to store.

Do I need to attach a shoe cabinet to the wall?

Tall, narrow furniture can tip. Follow any anti-tip instructions that come with the cabinet, and if you rent, check what is allowed.
