How to choose a large bread box for the countertop

  • Measure the counter space where the bread box will sit
  • Measure the height under the cabinets above it, so the lid or roll top can open
  • Check how many loaves the bread box is listed to hold
  • Decide whether you want a wooden box with a window, a vintage retro box or a metal box with a roll top
  • Check how the box opens, such as from the front or with a roll top
  • Check whether the top is flat enough to use for extra storage
