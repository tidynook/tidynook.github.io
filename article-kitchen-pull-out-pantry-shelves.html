How to measure a cabinet for a pull-out shelf

  • Measure the width of the cabinet opening at its narrowest point, since a door frame can reduce the space
  • Measure the depth from the front of the cabinet to the back wall
  • Check for hinges, pipes or a door that could block the drawer as it slides
  • Make sure the cabinet floor is flat and can hold the organizer
  • Think about what you will store, then compare it with the weight rating on the product page
  • Plan where each screw will go before you start, if the organizer needs fixing in place

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure a cabinet for a pull-out organizer?

Measure the opening width at its narrowest point, the depth of the cabinet, and note anything that could block the drawer, such as hinges or pipes. Then compare your numbers with the details on the product page.

Will a pull-out organizer fit a cabinet with a face frame?

A face frame can make the opening narrower than the inside of the cabinet. Measure the opening itself, not just the inside width.

Can I install a pull-out organizer myself?

Many include hardware and instructions. Read the installation steps on the product page and decide whether you are comfortable with a few screws before you order.
