How to stop drawer dividers from shifting

  • Measure the inside length of the drawer at its widest point
  • Pick dividers whose range covers that length with a little room to expand
  • Expand them until they press snugly against both sides, so there is no gap
  • Check that the divider height suits your drawer so lids and handles can close
  • If the drawer surface is slick, a non-slip liner underneath can add grip
  • Spread heavy items across compartments rather than loading one side

Frequently asked questions

Why do drawer dividers keep sliding?

The usual causes are a loose fit, a length range that does not match the drawer, or a slippery drawer surface. Expandable dividers that you adjust until they press firmly against the sides are designed to reduce this.

Are adjustable drawer dividers better than fixed ones?

Adjustable dividers fit more drawer sizes and can be tightened for a snug fit. Fixed dividers can work if they match your drawer exactly, but they leave no room for error.

How do I measure a drawer for dividers?

Measure the inside length, width and depth of the drawer, then compare those numbers with the length range and height listed on the product page.
