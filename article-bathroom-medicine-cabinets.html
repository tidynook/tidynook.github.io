How to choose a medicine cabinet with a mirror

  • Decide between a surface mounted cabinet, which sits on the wall, and a recessed one, which sits in it
  • Measure the wall space and the height of the spot where the mirror will hang
  • For a recessed cabinet, check the wall depth, the stud position and any pipes or wiring behind the wall
  • Check which way the door opens, so it does not hit the light, the sink or the wall
  • Think about what you will store, and compare it with the shelf details on the product page
  • If you rent, check what you are allowed to fix to the wall

What to check before buying

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a recessed and a surface mounted medicine cabinet?

A recessed cabinet sits inside the wall, so it needs space behind the wall. A surface mounted cabinet sits on the wall, so it is simpler to install.

Do I need to cut into the wall for a medicine cabinet?

Only if you choose to recess it. For a recessed cabinet, check the wall depth and anything behind the wall before you cut. A surface mounted cabinet does not need a hole.

Can I replace my old medicine cabinet myself?

Many people do, but check the installation steps on the product page, and bring in a professional if you are unsure about the wiring or plumbing behind the wall.
