How to choose a towel ladder

  • Measure the floor space and the wall height where the ladder will lean
  • Check that the base fits clear of the toilet, tub and door swing
  • Think about whether you want rungs for draping or hooks for hanging
  • Leave room to reach the towels without bumping the ladder
  • Do not overload the ladder with wet towels, and check any weight guidance on the product page
  • Check whether the page mentions how to keep the ladder steady against the wall

What to check before buying

  • Overall height and width against your wall space
  • Number of tiers and whether it has hooks
  • Weight guidance and stability notes
  • Assembly details on the Amazon page

Frequently asked questions

Does a wall-leaning towel ladder need drilling?

It is designed to lean against the wall, so it does not need to be mounted. Check the product page for any stability or safety notes.

Can I use a towel ladder in a small bathroom?

Yes, if you have a clear patch of wall and floor. Measure the space, and compare it with the overall size on the product page.

Can I use a blanket ladder for towels?

Yes. Many blanket ladders are also described as towel racks, so check the listing and the weight guidance.
