How to organize a linen closet

  • Empty the closet and sort items by type: towels, sheets, blankets and spare supplies
  • Measure each shelf for width, depth and the height to the shelf above
  • Fold towels and sheets the same way, so they stack evenly
  • Keep everyday items at eye level and rarely used items on the top shelf
  • Put heavier items on lower shelves, and light items on top
  • Label the shelves or bins, so everyone puts things back in the same place

Frequently asked questions

How do I organize a linen closet?

Sort items by type, measure your shelves, fold things the same way and keep everyday items at eye level. Then add drawers, bins or shelves where the space is wasted.

What size organizer do I need for my linen shelf?

Measure the width, depth and height of the shelf, then compare those numbers with the overall size on the product page.

Can I use a hanging organizer in a linen closet?

Yes, if the closet has a rod and enough drop. Check the overall size and the weight rating on the product page first.
