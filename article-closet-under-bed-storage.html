How to measure for under-bed storage

  • Measure from the floor to the lowest part of the bed frame, such as the side rails or slats
  • Measure in several spots, since centre legs and rails can block the way
  • Subtract a little room for wheels and lids so the bin slides out easily
  • Measure the depth from the edge of the bed to the wall or headboard
  • Compare your numbers with the height, length and width listed for the bin

What to check before buying

  • Bed clearance against the height of the bin
  • Whether the bed has centre support legs or slats in the way
  • Wheels or handles, so you can pull the bin out easily
  • The pack size and current details on the Amazon page
  • Recent buyer reviews on durability

Frequently asked questions

How much clearance do I need for under-bed storage?

You need more clearance than the height listed for the bin, with a little room to spare for wheels and lids. Measure from the floor to the lowest part of your bed frame.

Do under-bed bins fit every bed frame?

No. Clearance varies, and centre legs or low rails can block bins. Always compare your own measurements with the listed size.

Can I make room for taller bins?

Bed risers can add clearance, but check their weight rating and make sure they suit your bed before using them.

Do I need all 8 boxes in the Sterilite pack?

Not necessarily. They suit larger households or anyone organizing several rooms. If you only need one or two bins, the 2-pack alternative may be a better fit.
