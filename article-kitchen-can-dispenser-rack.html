How to choose a can dispenser rack for a pantry

  • Measure the width, depth and height of the shelf where the rack will sit
  • Check the height of your tallest cans against the tiers
  • Check how many cans each rack is listed to hold
  • Leave room to reach the cans without bumping the shelf above
  • Check how the tiers stack, and whether any assembly is needed
  • If you buy a 2 pack, measure the space for both units
