How to choose a hanging closet organizer

  • Measure the free space on the rod, and the drop from the rod to the floor or to the next shelf
  • Check that the hooks or loops fit around your rod
  • Think about what you will store, such as sweaters, scarves, linens or shoes
  • Put heavier items on the lower shelves for stability
  • Do not overload the shelves, and check the weight rating on the product page
  • If the organizer folds, you can store it flat when you do not need it

Frequently asked questions

What can I store in a hanging closet organizer?

Folded clothes such as sweaters and t-shirts, scarves, linens and small accessories suit it well. Keep heavier items on the lower shelves and stay within the weight rating.

Will a hanging closet organizer fit my closet?

Measure the free space on the rod and the drop below it, and compare them with the overall size on the product page.

Do hanging closet organizers need tools?

They hang from the rod, so usually no tools are needed. Check the setup details on the product page.
