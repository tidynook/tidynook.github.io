How to choose a garment rack

  • Measure the floor space and the ceiling height where the rack will stand
  • Hang a long coat or dress and measure it, so you know how much rod height you need
  • Add up what you plan to hang and compare it with the load rating on the product page
  • Choose a double rail or double rod if you want more hanging space without taking more floor
  • If the rack has wheels, check whether they lock before you load it
  • Leave room to walk past it and to open drawers or doors nearby

What to check before buying

Frequently asked questions

How much weight can a garment rack hold?

It depends on the model. Check the load rating on the product page and compare it with what you plan to hang, especially heavy coats.

Is a single rail or a double rail rack better?

A double rail gives more hanging space in the same footprint, which suits small rooms. A single rail is simpler if you hang fewer or longer items.

Do garment racks need assembly?

Most racks arrive in parts. Check the assembly details on the product page before you order.
