About Tidy Nook

Updated October 2026

Tidy Nook is a small guide site for organizing kitchens, closets and bathrooms, especially small, shared or rented spaces. The goal is simple: help you pick an organizer that actually fits before you order it.

Written by Muhammad Arif

I run Tidy Nook and write every guide on it. I am not a professional organizer or a lab. I read product specifications, check the measurements that matter (drawer depth, shelf height, door thickness, wall surface), and read what buyers say after using a product for a while. Then I tell you what to measure at home before you click buy.

Find me on LinkedIn or Pinterest.

How we pick and compare products

  • We start with a real problem, such as spices that are hard to see, no floor space in a closet, or a shower caddy that falls.
  • We compare products using the features and specifications on their Amazon pages and the feedback buyers leave in reviews.
  • We list what to check before buying, like size, weight rating and the surface it goes on.
  • We have not run lab tests on the products. Each guide says so, and asks you to confirm current details on the Amazon page.
