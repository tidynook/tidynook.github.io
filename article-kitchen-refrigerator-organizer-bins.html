How to choose a refrigerator organizer bins set

  • Measure the height, width and depth of the shelf where the bins will sit
  • Compare your measurements with the size of each bin on the product page
  • Check whether you want clear bins, bins with lids, or baskets for a freezer
  • Choose a pack size that matches the number of bins you will really use
  • Check how the bins stack, and whether lids add height
  • Keep similar foods together, so each bin has one job

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a refrigerator organizer bins set?

Measure your shelf height, width and depth, then compare them with the bin size on the product page. Choose the pack size that matches the number of bins you will use.

Are bins with lids a good idea for the fridge?

Lids can help keep contents together, but they add height, so measure your shelf before you order.

Can the same bins go in the fridge and the pantry?

Some bin sets are listed for both fridge and pantry use. Check each product page for where the bins are meant to go.
