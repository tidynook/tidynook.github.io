How to choose produce saver containers for the fridge

  • Measure the height, width and depth of the fridge shelf where the containers will sit
  • Compare your measurements with the container size on each product page
  • Decide whether you want plastic, glass or pull-out bins
  • Check whether a removable colander or a vent valve is listed
  • For vacuum seal containers, check which vacuum sealer they work with
  • Choose a pack size that matches how many containers you will really use
