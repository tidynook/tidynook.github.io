How to choose an over-the-door pantry organizer

  • Measure the door thickness, and compare it with what the hooks are made for
  • Measure the door height and width, and compare them with the overall size listed
  • Check the clearance between the door and the shelves inside the pantry, so the door can close
  • Think about what you will store, and keep heavy cans on the lower baskets
  • Check the weight rating for each basket on the product page
  • Decide whether you want baskets you can adjust, so tall items fit

What to check before buying

Frequently asked questions

Will an over-the-door pantry organizer fit my door?

Measure your door thickness, height and width, and compare them with the details on the product page. Also check the clearance inside the pantry so the door can close.

Can an over-the-door organizer damage my door?

Check what the product page says about the hooks and any padding. Hanging items on a door can add wear, so check the door thickness and the weight rating before you load it.

What can I store on a pantry door organizer?

Spices, cans, snacks and small packets suit it well. Keep heavier items on the lower baskets, and stay within the weight rating.
